Alfama Walks

Alfama Walks is a made-up company for the Seekwide eval suite. It runs small-group walking tours through the oldest quarter of Lisbon, with at most ten people per guide, starting every morning at the cathedral steps.

Each walk takes about three hours, follows the stairways and lanes between the castle and the river, and ends at a viewpoint over the rooftops. See the tours or book a place.
