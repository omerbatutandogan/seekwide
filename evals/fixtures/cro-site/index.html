Harbor Lane Plumbing

Harbor Lane Plumbing is a made-up plumbing firm for the Seekwide eval suite. It repairs leaks, replaces boilers and clears drains for homes and small offices in the harbour district. Two vans are on the road six days a week, and most repairs are done on the first visit.

Prices are fixed before the work starts. A call-out within the district costs the same at any hour, and the invoice lists every part.

Call 1-800-PLUMBER or send us a message.
