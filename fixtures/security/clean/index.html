Harbor Coffee Roasters

We roast small batches of coffee every morning in a converted boathouse at the edge of the harbour, and we serve it in the café upstairs with bread from the bakery next door.

Our beans come from three cooperatives that we visit every year. We pay them above the market price and publish what we pay, so that you know where your money goes.

The café opens at seven. Bring your own cup and the first refill is on us.

Harbor Coffee Roasters · 12 Quay Street · Open daily from seven until six
