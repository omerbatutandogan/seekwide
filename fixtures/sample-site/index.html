Harbor Coffee Roasters

We roast small batches of coffee every morning in a converted boathouse at the edge of the harbour, and we serve it in the café upstairs with bread from the bakery next door. The roaster is a twelve-kilo drum machine that we restored ourselves, and on most days you can watch it work through the window by the stairs.

Our beans come from three cooperatives that we visit every year. We pay them well above the market price and publish what we pay, so that you know where your money goes. Each lot is roasted lighter than most cafés would dare, to keep the fruit and the sweetness that the farmers worked for.

The café opens at seven. Bring your own cup and the first refill is on us. For beans, filters and the grinder we use behind the bar, click here. Restaurants and offices can ask about our wholesale prices.
