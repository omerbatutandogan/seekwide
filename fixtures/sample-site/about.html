About us

Harbor Coffee Roasters started in 2019, when two friends who had worked behind other people's bars for ten years found an empty boathouse with a leaking roof and a landlord who liked coffee. The first year we roasted on a borrowed one-kilo machine and sold beans from a table by the door.

Today we are a team of nine. Mara runs the roastery and cups every batch before it is bagged. Teo runs the café and trains every new barista for two weeks before they work a shift alone. The rest of us do a bit of everything, from packing orders to fixing the espresso machine when it sulks on cold mornings.

We believe that good coffee should be honest about where it comes from. That is why every bag names the farm, the cooperative, the harvest month and the price we paid. If you have a question about any of it, ask at the bar or write to us.
