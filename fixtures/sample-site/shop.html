Shop

Every coffee we roast is for sale in 250 gram and one kilo bags, whole bean or ground for your brewer. We roast to order twice a week and post the same day, so the beans you receive were roasted no more than four days before they reach you.

Alongside the beans you will find paper filters, the hand grinder we use for cupping, a glass pour-over set and our enamel mugs, which were made for us by a potter up the coast. A subscription brings a different single origin to your door every two or four weeks, and you can pause it whenever you go away.

Shipping is free above forty euros. If a coffee is not to your taste, tell us within a month and we will send you another one, no questions asked. Orders placed before noon leave the roastery the same afternoon.
