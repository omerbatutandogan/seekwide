Visit and contact

The roastery and the café share the old boathouse at 12 Quay Street, at the far end of the harbour past the fish market. The café is on the first floor; the roastery is on the ground floor, and you can usually look in when the door is open.

We are open every day from seven in the morning until six in the evening, and until eight on Fridays in summer. The café closes on the first of January and on the twenty-fifth of December, when the roaster gets its yearly clean.

For questions about an order, a wholesale account or a coffee you bought from us, write to the address on the back of your bag, or ask whoever is behind the bar. We answer every message within two working days, usually faster.
