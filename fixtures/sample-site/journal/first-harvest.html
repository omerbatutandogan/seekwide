The first harvest from the new cooperative

In March a sample bag arrived from a cooperative of forty families who farm on steep slopes three hours from the nearest town. They had never sold abroad before, and the bag came with a handwritten note asking us to be honest about what we thought. We cupped it the same morning with the whole team.

It tasted of red apple and brown sugar, with a long, clean finish that kept going after the cup was cold. We bought the whole lot, twenty-two bags, before lunch. We paid a little over twice the market price that day, and the cooperative will use part of it to build a drying bed with a roof, so that the next harvest does not depend on the weather.

The coffee is in the shop now as a filter roast, and on the pour-over bar in the café until it runs out. If you try it, tell us what you taste; we will pass every note on to the farmers.
