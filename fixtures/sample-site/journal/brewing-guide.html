A brewing guide for the hand filter

You need fifteen grams of coffee for a mug of two hundred and fifty grams of water. Grind it about as coarse as sea salt; if the cup tastes bitter next time, grind a little coarser, and if it tastes sour and thin, a little finer. Heat the water to just below boiling and let it rest for thirty seconds.

Rinse the paper filter with hot water and pour that water away. Add the coffee, shake it flat and pour just enough water to wet all of it. Wait thirty seconds while it bubbles: that is the gas from the roast escaping, and it is why fresh coffee needs this step. Then pour the rest slowly, in circles, over about a minute and a half.

The water should have drained through by three and a half to four minutes. If it takes much longer, the grind is too fine; if it rushes through, too coarse. Change one thing at a time, write it down, and within a week you will brew a better cup than most cafés.
