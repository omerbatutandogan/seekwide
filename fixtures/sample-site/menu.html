Our menu

Espresso drinks are made with our house blend, which changes with the seasons and is roasted on Mondays and Thursdays. Filter coffee is a single origin that we brew by the cup on a pour-over bar, and the barista will happily tell you about the farm it comes from while you wait.

We also serve loose-leaf tea from a small importer in the old town, hot chocolate made with real chocolate and oat milk at no extra cost. Pastries come from the bakery next door every morning at half past six, and the cardamom buns are usually gone by ten, so come early if you want one.

Prices are on the board above the bar. Everything can be taken away in a paper cup, or in your own cup for a small discount. We do not take reservations, but there is almost always a seat by the window if you are patient for a minute or two.
